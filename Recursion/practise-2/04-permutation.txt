What is permutation?

Arranging items over a line

Without repetetion:
Suppose we are arranging people over a line same person can't repeated
hennce formulae is p(n,r) 
Where n is total people available and r is number i am chosing 

With repetetion
Suppose take number plate, repetetion allowed how many ways i can 
arrange 'n' number over 'r' slots?
n*power(r)

Combination:

Selecting the items,

Without repetetion 
Suppose i am selecting r people out of total n people.
Then how many ways i can select: C(n,r) = n!/r!*(n-r)!

With repetetion:
Suppose i am selecting fruits with repeated values 
there are n different fruits and i want to select r fruits
repetetion allowed

C(n+r-1,r) is the formulae = (n+r-1)!/r!(n-1)!
